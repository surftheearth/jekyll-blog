- In another life I was born somewhere else.
- In another life I attend hogwarts and magic exists.
- In another life I’d live differently my teenage years.
- In another life him and I would be legendary lovers.
- In another life…

![_config.yml]({{ site.baseurl }}/images/config.png)

“In another life”… How can solely three words bring so much comfort and peace?
How can they be so comforting for a person who does not believe in reincarnation?
How can they comfort us so deeply if, when we stop to think about it, we can easily realise how unimaginable and impossible our ideas can be?

In THIS life, in OUR life we have limited time, therefore we can’t have eight different degrees, we can’t have more than one love of our life, we can’t be from more than our own country, we can’t have more than seven years of teenagehood, we can’t have a home and a life in all the cities we’d like to.

But most importantly we can’t have a second go to live our life. We can’t reach the end of our lives and ask for a re-do. There’s ONE go. No ‘another’ no ‘in my next’. This one; now; todays and tomorrows. Those are the rules. Scary undoubtedly.

That’s probably the reason why we resort to believing, because unwillingly or willingly we do believe; deeply or superficially, that the possibility of our dreams coming true exists when we say ‘In another life’. Maybe only because we could not possible live, nor go to sleep at peace, nor carry the burden of the impossibility, of our impotence towards making a dream of ours come true: big or small. Marrying a celebrity or having a date with a school crush, being a world champion or simply play and be friends with the class sporty guys.
Because we always feel the need to be in control of everything. even when we can’t do absolutely nothing. We find it very hard to accept that. I think few people ever accept that.

And, well; Of course it hurts. It f*cking STINGS. And it’s terribly scary because we don’t feel like our feelings are supported, we’re naked in the middle of Times Square. 
But that’s okay. There’s beauty in not being able to fully control our lives: it’s exciting and

I mean life is in everything: life is in sun rays passing through green spring leaves, life is in running clear water, life is in every connection we form with another human being, life is in all of us and we can’t control that. Life occurs and we get to create our own story, but of course only as script helpers of life itself.

So be excited about your reality; it doesn’t matter if you’re hitting rock bottom or if you’re in what you think are your best years because life goes on; you’re alive! And that’s the biggest blessing ever.
